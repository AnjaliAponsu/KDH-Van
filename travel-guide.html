Travel Tips for Visiting Sri Lanka

Make the most of your trip with these practical travel tips, from the best time to visit to getting around comfortably.

Best Time to Visit

Generally December to April for beaches, and May to September for hill country.

Getting Around

Travel comfortably with a private van and driver for a safe, flexible and hassle-free trip.

What to Pack

Light, breathable clothing, a light jacket for the hills, and sun protection.

Travel Etiquette

Respect local customs, dress modestly at religious sites, and be mindful of culture.

Stay Safe

Sri Lanka is a welcoming destination. Keep your belongings secure and follow local advice.
