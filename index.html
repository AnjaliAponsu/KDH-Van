FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our KDH van rentals and driver services.

Is a driver included with the KDH van?

Yes, every rental includes an experienced, professional, English-speaking driver certified by the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority. For passenger safety, local traffic familiarity, and insurance compliance, we do not provide self-drive van rentals.

How is the per-kilometre / route price calculated?

Route fares are based on point-to-point journey distance utilizing modern expressways. All quotations are transparent and all-inclusive: vehicle rental, driver fee, fuel, expressway tolls, and passenger liability insurance. There are zero hidden fees.

Can I arrange an airport pickup at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA)?

Yes, airport pickups and drop-offs are our core specialty. Your assigned chauffeur will be waiting inside the arrival paging hall with a personalized name-board to assist you with luggage directly to your air-conditioned KDH van.

What happens if my incoming flight is delayed?

Flight delays are covered completely free of charge. We monitor all incoming flight schedules via live airport radar tracking and adjust your driver's arrival time automatically without waiting charges.

How do I confirm or cancel my booking?

You can submit your trip request online or directly via WhatsApp. Once availability is confirmed, you receive an official booking reservation. We offer 100% free cancellation up to 24 hours before your scheduled pickup time.
