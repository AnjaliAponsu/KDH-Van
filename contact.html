COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding bookings, airport meet & greets, chauffeur services, and payment options.

How quickly will I receive confirmation for my inquiry?

For WhatsApp messages, our on-duty concierge typically replies within 3 to 5 minutes 24/7. Inquiries submitted through our website contact form or email receive comprehensive official quotations and vehicle availability within 30 to 60 minutes.

Is an English-speaking chauffeur included with every rental?

Yes, 100% of our hires include a licensed, highly experienced English-speaking professional chauffeur certified by the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority. To maintain supreme passenger safety and full vehicle insurance protection, we do not provide self-drive van rentals.

What is included in the van rental quotation?

All quotes are completely transparent with zero hidden charges. Rates include the Toyota KDH 223 High Roof commuter van, professional chauffeur allowance, air-conditioning, fuel for agreed itineraries, expressway tolls, passenger insurance, and complimentary bottled water.

What happens if my inbound flight to Colombo (BIA) is delayed?

Flight tracking is included free of charge. We monitor incoming flights at Bandaranaike International Airport in real-time. If your flight is delayed or arrives early, your chauffeur automatically adjusts arrival time without waiting penalty fees.

What payment methods and currencies do you accept?

We accept major international currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD) as well as Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR). Payment can be completed via secure online payment link, credit/debit card, bank transfer, or cash upon arrival with receipt.
