South Coast Travel Guide – Galle & Mirissa

A complete guide to Sri Lanka's stunning South Coast — from the historic Galle Fort to the palm-fringed beaches of Mirissa, with top places to see, things to do, and practical travel tips for a perfect coastal getaway.

Why Visit the South Coast

Sri Lanka's South Coast perfectly blends history, culture and beach life. Explore the colonial charm of Galle, relax on sun-kissed beaches, enjoy world-class surfing, spot whales in Mirissa, and soak in a laid-back coastal atmosphere with great food, friendly locals and stunning ocean views.

Quick Facts: South Coast

Main areas

Galle, Unawatuna, Mirissa, Weligama

Best time

Dec – Apr (best weather & calm seas)

Travel time

≈ 2 – 2.5 hours from Colombo

Ideal trip

Day trip or 2–3 day coastal getaway

Top experiences

Galle Fort, beaches, surfing, whale watching, sea turtle spotting, coastal dining

Trip Planning Tips

Make the most of your South Coast trip with these handy tips.

  • Allow 2–3 days to explore Galle, beaches and Mirissa at a relaxed pace.
  • Start early from Colombo to enjoy more time at each stop.
  • Book whale watching in advance during the season (Nov – Apr).
  • Bring swimwear, sunscreen, a hat and a reusable water bottle.
  • Travel with a professional driver for a safe, comfortable and stress-free coastal experience.

Sample South Coast Itinerary (2 Days)

A relaxed coastal getaway covering history, beaches and ocean experiences.

Day 1 Colombo → Galle Fort (explore & lunch) → Unawatuna (beach & snorkeling) → Overnight in Galle or Unawatuna
Day 2 Mirissa (whale watching – seasonal) → Weligama (surfing or beach time) → Return to Colombo
