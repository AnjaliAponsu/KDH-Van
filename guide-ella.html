Ella Travel Guide

Your complete guide to visiting Ella — top attractions, scenic spots, travel tips, itineraries and everything you need to plan a memorable trip with a comfortable KDH van and a professional driver.

★ Why Visit Ella

A charming hill-country town, Ella is famous for its breathtaking scenery, tea plantations, iconic train views, waterfalls and laid-back vibe. It's the perfect blend of nature, adventure and relaxation, making it one of Sri Lanka's most loved destinations.

Ravana Falls

→

A beautiful cascade just a short drive from Ella, perfect for a refreshing stop.

📅 Best Scenic Stops

Don't miss these beautiful spots when visiting Ella.

  • Ella Rock (a scenic and rewarding hike)
  • Demodara Loop (unique spiral railway)
  • Upper Diyaluma / Diyaluma Falls (stunning views)
  • Halpewatte Tea Factory (learn about Ceylon tea)
  • Kithal Ella Viewpoint (a peaceful, lesser-known spot)

📅 When to Visit

Ella is beautiful year-round, but the best time is during the drier months.

Best Time: Dec – Mar & Jul – Sep
  • Pleasant, cooler weather
  • Clearer views and great for hiking
  • Ideal for outdoor activities and photography

ⓘ Travel Advice

Helpful tips to make your Ella trip smooth and enjoyable.

  • Wear comfortable shoes for hikes.
  • Carry a light jacket (it can get cool, especially evenings).
  • Keep a camera or phone ready for scenic views.
  • Start hikes early for the best weather and fewer crowds.
  • Travel with a reliable van and driver for a safe, comfortable and flexible trip with KDH Van Hire.
